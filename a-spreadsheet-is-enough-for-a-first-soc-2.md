# A Spreadsheet Is Enough For A First SOC 2

## Stance

For a company of about 14 people getting its first SOC 2 Type I, a well-kept control matrix spreadsheet and a structured evidence folder are enough. A compliance automation platform is not needed to pass, and buying one first teaches the team to satisfy a tool rather than understand the controls.

## Scope

First report, small team, one production system. Not a claim about Type II at scale or about companies with several products.

## Why I Think This

- Our Type I went through on the [[soc2-control-matrix]] and the [[soc2-evidence-folder]], at $0 in tooling.
- Writing each control by hand forced owners to understand it. [[colette-fairbanks]] rewrote the offboarding rows three times and now knows them better than any template would.
- [[patrice-ndour]] said the same at kickoff: auditors test evidence, not dashboards.

## What Would Change My Mind

Status is Revising because the Type II has started to test it. Both Type I exceptions were recurring controls nobody was reminded of. If evidence collection in 2026-09 to 2026-12 costs more than about 8 hours a month between Colette and me, or if any occurrence is missed, a tool is worth its $12,000 to $20,000 a year.

## Implications

Keep the spreadsheet through the Type II window either way. Decide on a tool for the next cycle with the 2027 budget.
