# Sigrid Aaltonen

Former CTO of a B2B software company that she took from seed to about 120 people, now an angel investor. Informal technical advisor to me since 2024; not an investor in Quaylane.

## How We Work Together

- Coffee or a video call roughly every six weeks, which I set up. No fee, no formal role.
- I bring one specific decision each time and she asks questions until I can state my own answer. She rarely gives a recommendation outright.

## Conversations Worth Remembering

- 2025-12: after [[architecture-review-2025-11-18]] she asked what Meridale would have accepted if we had asked them directly. I had not asked. That question sat with me until the reversal.
- 2026-05-26: before [[architecture-review-2026-06-11]] she said reversals are cheap if you announce them early and expensive if you let a project drift. That shaped how I ran the review: numbers first, decision in the room, written record the same week ([[reversals-should-be-cheap-and-public]]).
- 2026-10: on platform engineering, she hired her first platform engineer at about 25 engineers and thinks a 7-engineer team should first remove toil, then hire. Notes in [[platform-engineering]].

## Boundaries

She knows no customer names beyond what is public and sees no company documents. I keep the conversation at the level of decisions and trade-offs.
