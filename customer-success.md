# Customer success

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-19.

## Summary

Describes customer success as a business strategy and organizational function aimed at raising the value customers get from a product so they stay, common in subscription software where recurring revenue depends on ongoing satisfaction.

## Why It Matters

Renata hired Imani in 2024 to build this function, and it shapes how engineering and customer success split work: Imani's team owns onboarding, renewals and the [[customer-feedback-loop]]; engineering owns escalations that need code or data. The article's link between customer value and churn is the reason the feedback log has a churn-risk tag.

## Caveats

Describes the function in general; it says nothing about team size for a company of 280 small accounts.
