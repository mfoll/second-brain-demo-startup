# Booking Widget Is A Different Product

## Stance

Patient self-booking is a second product with a different user (the patient), a different risk surface (public and unauthenticated) and a different support load. Building it inside Quaylane's roadmap as one theme among three underestimates it.

## Scope

The [[online-booking-widget]] as currently framed. A narrow version for returning patients with a link sent by the clinic would be much closer to the existing product.

## Why I think this

- Every other Quaylane surface is used by trained clinic staff. The widget would be used by anyone with a browser.
- It brings abuse cases we have never handled: bulk fake bookings, slot hoarding, framing on third-party websites.
- Support load: patients will contact the clinic, the clinic will contact us.

## What would change my mind

Felix's framing in [[front-desk-jobs-to-be-done]] has already moved me: the job is the front desk's, and the widget removes phone calls from their day. If the estimate review shows a minimal, link-based version at under 12 weeks, I would support it. Status is Revising for that reason.
