# Structured interview

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-08-03.

## Summary

A structured interview presents every respondent with exactly the same questions in the same order, so that answers can be aggregated reliably and compared across groups or periods. The article describes it mainly as a survey research method.

## Why It Matters

We borrow the principle, not the survey method: each candidate for a role gets the same questions per stage from the [[interview-question-bank]], and interviewers score before the debrief ([[run-an-engineering-interview-loop]]). It made the 2026-02 debrief comparable across four finalists ([[hiring-debrief-2026-02-20]]).

## Caveats

The article is about research interviews; the evidence on structured job interviews is a separate literature I have not ingested.
