# The Influences of Workload on the Work Quality of Agile Software Development Teams

Sanden C, Karnowski K, Steinke M, Neumann M, Linke L. HMD Praxis der Wirtschaftsinformatik, 2022. Published in German; title as indexed in English.

## Summary

A study of workload factors among agile software development team members at one large company after the shift to remote work. The authors identify factors behind high workload, propose measures, and test some in an experiment. Even small changes, such as planned rest phases during the working day, improved reported concentration and, through it, the quality of work results.

## Why It Matters

I read it in 2026-06, after the spring when Teodora and Kwabena carried the tenant migration and the outage clean-up while the rest of the team pushed for a June release. The version 3 slip was partly a workload problem disguised as a planning problem. It supports the case for a fixed platform reservation in [[roadmap-2027]] rather than squeezing platform work into product time.

## Key Details

- Single company, one experiment; qualitative and quantitative measures.

## Caveats

One organization, different scale and culture from ours. I take the direction, not the effect size.
