# On-Call Stipend Decision Memo

Two-page memo I wrote for [[renata-castellano]] and [[colette-fairbanks]], approved on 2026-04-10.

## Summary

A flat $300 for each primary on-call week, paid through payroll the following month. Budget impact about $15,600 a year.

## Options considered

- Flat weekly stipend: chosen. Predictable, does not reward noisy alerts.
- Per-page payment: rejected; pays more for worse alerting.
- Time off in lieu only: rejected; in a team of seven it rarely gets taken.
- No compensation, as in 2025: rejected; the 2025-11 survey scored on-call 2.6 of 5.

## Caveats

The secondary week is unpaid. Raised again in 2026-06 ([[should-the-secondary-on-call-week-be-paid]]).
