# Career Ladder Draft

Draft v3 of the engineering ladder, the text discussed at [[career-ladder-calibration-2026-10-14]].

## Summary

Five levels on four dimensions. Each cell has two to four sentences and, from v4, two Quaylane examples.

## Key Details

| Level | Name | Scope in one line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | Associate engineer | Delivers well-defined tasks with support |
| E2 | Engineer | Delivers features independently within one area |
| E3 | Senior engineer | Owns an area end to end, including its on-call behavior |
| E4 | Staff engineer | Shapes technical direction across areas without needing reports |
| E5 | Principal engineer | Sets technical strategy with the founders; rare |

Dimensions: technical scope, delivery, collaboration, operational ownership (on-call, incidents, security).

## Caveats

Describes levels, not people. Placement is decided in the review cycle and kept in the HR system. No pay bands in this document.
