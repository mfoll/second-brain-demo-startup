## How we work together

Hamish says what he thinks in the meeting rather than afterwards, which I value and want more of on this team. He prefers a decision explained in writing; when I made the code review call I sent him the reasoning the same afternoon. Our one-to-ones are every second Thursday.

## Positions on record

- **Code review and pairing.** At [[engineering-meeting-2026-04-08]] he argued that pair-programmed commits should count as reviewed: two engineers at one keyboard is more review than a third person approving a diff. The team kept the rule of one recorded approval because the auditor ([[patrice-ndour]]) accepts only that as evidence. Hamish still disagrees and asked that it be recorded. He follows the rule and records his pair as co-author anyway. See [[should-pair-programmed-commits-count-as-reviewed]].
- **AI assistants.** Thinks they make pairing more valuable, not less, because someone has to read what the assistant wrote.
