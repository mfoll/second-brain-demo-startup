# Should Release Dates Be Promised To Customers?

## Question

After telling every account that 3.0 would arrive on 2026-06-23 and then moving it to 2026-09-15, should we give customers release dates at all, and if so, how far ahead?

## Current Answer

> [!info]
> Only contractual dates are promised. Everything else is shown as a half-year until it is inside a six-week window, when Lucia's release train makes the date reliable enough to share.

Agreed at the [[roadmap-offsite-2026-10-14]] by Renata, Felix and me. Imani's team uses the same rule in customer calls.

## Why

- The June date cost us credibility with about a dozen accounts that had planned training around it. Imani's team spent most of 2026-06 on those calls.
- The SSO date for Meridale held because it was treated as a contract, with its own plan and slack. That is the difference.
- Sales still needs something to say. "First half 2027" is enough for small clinics; enterprise prospects get contractual dates or nothing.
