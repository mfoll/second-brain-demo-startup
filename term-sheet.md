# Term Sheet

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-05-27.

## Summary

A term sheet is a condensed, bulleted outline of the key conditions of a prospective deal, the starting point for negotiation and often the first written record of it. Once signed it guides the lawyers drafting the final agreements, but it does not strictly bind the parties while the remaining details are worked out. It is closely related to a letter of intent.

## Why It Matters

Read the day after Renata announced the signed term sheet ([[term-sheet-signed-2026-09-30]]), when two engineers asked whether the round was "done". The honest answer: the terms are agreed and the close is expected on 2026-11-20, but until then the money is not in the bank.
