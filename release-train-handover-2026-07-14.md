# Release Train Handover 2026-07-14

[[lucia-ferreyra|Lucia Ferreyra]] took over the release train: the weekly release cut on Tuesday, the release notes, and the go or no-go call.

## What changed

- Before: I cut every release, wrote the notes and decided whether to ship. Since 2025 that was about three hours a week, and when I was away nothing shipped.
- After: Lucia owns the train at level 4 on the [[levels-of-delegation]] scale: she decides and tells me. I am consulted only when a release touches tenant isolation or a Meridale contractual item.
- [[felix-ostrowski]] agreed the product side: he gives Lucia the scope by Monday noon.

## Why now

The slip of version 3 to 2026-09-15 ([[release-planning-2026-06-02]]) meant two and a half months of releases that each carried pieces of 3.0. Lucia was already running the calendar work and understood the feature flags better than I did.

## First results

Ten weekly releases from 2026-07-14 to 2026-09-15, plus the 3.0 GA. One rolled back (2026-08-11, a calendar rendering regression), and Lucia made that call without asking me. That was the point.
