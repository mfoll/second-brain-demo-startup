# Penetration Test

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-23.

## Summary

A penetration test is an authorized simulated cyberattack on a computer system, performed live to evaluate its security: to find weaknesses that could let unauthorized parties reach the system's features and data, and to identify strengths, so that a full risk assessment can be made.

## Why It Matters

The "authorized" and "simulated" parts are what I explain to customers when they ask why we let someone attack Quaylane. Our 2026 test by [[ivo-radic]] ([[bracken-hollow-pen-test-report-2026]]) is evidence for SOC 2 control VM-03 and for the tenant isolation claim. The 2027 test is in [[security-roadmap-2027]].
