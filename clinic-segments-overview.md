## How I read it

- No segment dominates, which is good for resilience and bad for focus. Recall reminders would serve dental and physio, nearly half of ARR, which is why recall made the [[roadmap-2027-draft]].
- Meridale alone is larger than imaging. Concentration risk is discussed in [[meridale-clinic-group]].
