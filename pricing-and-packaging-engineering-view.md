# Pricing And Packaging, Engineering View

What each plan costs us to run and support, as input to Renata's pricing decisions. Not the price list, which Renata and Dana own.

## Plans in 2026

- **Starter**: one site, up to 5 admin users, 1,500 SMS reminders a month included (raised from 1,000 in 2025-10 after the [[ezra-feldman-churn-call-2025-08-14]]).
- **Practice**: up to 5 sites, waitlist autofill, multi-location calendars.
- **Enterprise**: SSO, dedicated database on request, contractual availability, onboarding fee.

## Cost drivers I track

- SMS through Pingrove is the largest variable cost, about 40% of cloud and vendor spend per small account.
- Cloud cost per clinic is about $14 a month on average, the number Arun Velayudhan asked about at the [[technical-diligence-call-2026-08-19]]. A dedicated enterprise database costs far more per tenant, which the enterprise plan has to cover.
- Support time: small accounts generate about three times as many tickets per admin user as practice accounts.
