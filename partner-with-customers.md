# Partner With Customers

How engineering shows up for the clinics that pay us: customer feedback, the Meridale enterprise account and escalations that customer success cannot close alone. It never ends because the customers do not.

## Scope

- Feedback intake and the monthly product review, run with [[imani-brooks|Imani Brooks]] and [[felix-ostrowski|Felix Ostrowski]]. The working record is the [[customer-feedback-log]].
- The [[meridale-clinic-group]] account: the engineering commitments in the contract, the rollout, and the quarterly business review.
- Escalations from customer success: anything that needs an engineer within a day, a data fix, or an apology from a founder.
- Not here: security questionnaires and audits (under [[secure-and-comply]]), incident handling itself (under [[run-the-platform]], via [[run-an-incident]]), sales pipeline (Dana and Renata).

## Current State

- About 280 clinic accounts and 1,900 admin users. Physiotherapy and dental are the two largest segments; see [[clinic-segments-overview]].
- Meridale: all three contract conditions met (SSO 2026-06-30, dedicated database 2026-08-21, SOC 2 Type I 2026-08-28). Rollout at 19 of 42 clinics; target 2026-12-11. See [[meridale-commitments-tracker]].
- Feedback volume in 2026 runs at about 140 items a month. Online booking is the most requested item for the third quarter in a row.
- Two smaller customers still remember the 2026-03-17 outage when they talk to us; [[hana-kovarik|Hana Kovarik]] mentions it in every call.
