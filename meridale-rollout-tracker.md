# Meridale Rollout Tracker

The shared spreadsheet that tracks each of Meridale's 42 clinics through the rollout, kept by Imani's team with engineering columns filled by Lucia.

## Summary

- One row per clinic: wave, go-live date, site configuration done, SSO users synced, test import status, final import counts, training dates, hypercare issues.
- Status on 2026-10-21: 19 live, 12 in wave 3 preparation, 11 in wave 4.

## Why It Matters

It is the single place to answer "where is clinic X?" for Victoria Ashdown's team and ours, and the source for [[can-meridale-finish-rollout-by-december]].

## Key Details

- Wave 3 test imports: 9 of 12 done, 3 waiting on a new export format.
- Imported future appointments to date: about 98,000 across waves 1 and 2, zero rejects in final imports.
- Hypercare issues: 23 (wave 1), 7 (wave 2).

## Caveats

Counts only; no appointment details are kept in the sheet.
