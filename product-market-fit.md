# Product–market fit

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-26.

## Summary

A short article defining product–market fit as the degree to which a product satisfies strong demand in a market.

## Why It Matters

Margot Ellison asked at the 2026-05 board meeting whether Quaylane has product–market fit with clinic groups or only with small clinics. Renata's answer was that small clinics show it (low churn outside closures, steady inbound) and clinic groups are one data point, Meridale. I keep the article linked from [[product-discovery]] as a reminder that the [[roadmap-2027]] clinic group theme is a bet on a segment we have not proven yet.

## Caveats

A definition, not a measurement method. Our evidence for fit lives in the [[churn-analysis-2025]] and the feedback log.
