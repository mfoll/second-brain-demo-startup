# How I Prepare For Board Meetings

What I have settled on after three years of quarterly board meetings as a co-founder. At my previous employer I presented to directors, never to a board; the difference is that here the board can change what we do next quarter.

## Two Weeks Before

- Draft the engineering page: one slide, five bullets maximum, numbers with dates. Lead with anything that went wrong.
- Every decision that went to an architecture review gets a cost line, a request from Margot after [[board-meeting-2026-06-16]].
- Send the draft to Renata. She cuts about a third.

## One Week Before

- Read the last two meetings' notes for promises I made ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]] promised a rollout estimate I should have revisited).
- Prepare answers to the two questions Margot is most likely to ask. So far she has always asked about risk or cost, never features.

## After

- Write an Event note the same day with decisions and anything I promised.
- Engineering-relevant decisions go to the team in the next Tuesday sync.

## Note

Renata and I agreed not to disagree in a board meeting. If we have not resolved something, it goes on the agenda as an open question with both positions, as we will do for the first 2027 hire on 2026-12-08.
