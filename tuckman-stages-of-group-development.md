# Tuckman's stages of group development

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-11.

## Summary

The forming, storming, norming and performing model of group development, first proposed by Bruce Tuckman in 1965. He argued that these phases are necessary for a team to grow, face challenges, solve problems and deliver results.

## Why It Matters

A rough lens for 2026: two new engineers, an outage, a contested review rule and a slipped release in six months. The spring felt like storming, and the [[team-working-agreements]] revised in 2026-07 were a norming step. Useful for expecting friction after each hire rather than reading it as a problem.

## Caveats

A descriptive model, not a measurement. I use it for vocabulary only.
