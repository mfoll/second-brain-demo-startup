# Renata Castellano

CEO and co-founder of Quaylane. Before Quaylane she ran operations for a group of outpatient clinics, which is where the product idea came from: she spent years watching front-desk staff juggle paper waitlists and three calendars. We founded the company together in April 2023 with the $2.0M seed led by Larchgate Ventures.

## Role

Renata owns the company: strategy, fundraising, the board, sales leadership and the company budget. She is the person clinic operators trust on sight, and she still sits in on customer calls with practice managers. I own engineering and the technical story; [[co-founder-split-with-renata]] records how we divided the rest.

## How We Work Together

- Weekly co-founder one-to-one on Mondays, 60 minutes, with a shared running doc. She brings customer and money questions, I bring team and platform questions.
- She asks for decisions in one paragraph with a recommendation; long technical write-ups go to the vault and I send her the link.
- We disagree openly in the one-to-one and present one position to the team and the board. The tenant isolation decision of 2025-11-18 was one we debated: she wanted the simplest answer for Meridale's security team, I worried about cost. We both underestimated the migration fan-out.
- On fundraising she leads and I answer. She prefers me on investor calls only for diligence, not for pitch meetings.
