# Usability testing

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-11.

## Summary

Describes usability testing, a user-centered design technique in which a product is evaluated by having real users try it, with attention to how intuitive it is for people who have not used it before, as distinct from expert inspection methods without users.

## Why It Matters

Mai's practice for every major screen. For [[multi-location-calendars]] she tested three site switcher variants with front-desk staff at two Meridale pilot clinics; the variant engineering liked least won. Recordings are linked from the [[quaylane-design-library]]. I keep this article as the plain definition I give engineers when Mai asks them to watch a session.

## Caveats

Our tests use three to five users per round, enough to catch big problems, not to compare variants statistically.
