# Career Ladder

Write engineering levels so that promotions and the December reviews rest on something other than my memory.

## Outcome

Engineering levels E1 to E5 written, calibrated with the CEO and used in the December 2026 reviews.

## Current State

- Draft v3 of [[career-ladder-draft]] covers E1 (associate engineer) to E5 (principal engineer) on four dimensions: technical scope, delivery, collaboration, and operational ownership.
- Calibration session with [[renata-castellano]] on 2026-10-14 agreed the E2/E3 boundary and asked for clearer E4 examples ([[career-ladder-calibration-2026-10-14]]).
- The ladder describes levels, not people. Who sits at which level is decided in the review cycle and kept in the HR system.
- A separate manager track is deferred until we have a second manager ([[should-the-career-ladder-have-a-separate-manager-track]]).

## Scope and Boundaries

The ladder text and its calibration. Out of scope: pay bands (Renata and [[colette-fairbanks]]), and individual placement.

## Working Context

I started from the ladder I used at my previous employer and cut it by two thirds. The things a 14-person company needs that the big-company ladder lacked: on-call and incident ownership as a first-class dimension, and customer contact. Teodora and Kwabena reviewed v2 in August; their main ask was that E4 not require "leading a team", since we have no teams.
