# System And Organization Controls: SOC Suite Of Services

Resource page published by AICPA & CIMA on the SOC family of services.

## Summary

The page presents SOC as a set of services CPAs can provide, reporting on controls at a service organization's system level or an entity level, to help users of those services manage the risks of outsourcing. It lists publications including the SOC 2 guide on controls relevant to Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality or Privacy. At the time I read it, the page also carried a notice that the AICPA was looking into allegations about one SOC compliance vendor's practices and urged careful evaluation of SOC services.

## Why It Matters

It is the primary publisher behind the framework our [[soc2-type-1-report]] follows, and the link I give team members who ask "who decides what SOC 2 is?". The notice about a compliance vendor is a small input to [[should-we-buy-a-compliance-automation-platform]]: whatever tool we pick, the audit firm's independence matters more than speed.

## Caveats

The page is an overview and does not explain Type I versus Type II; for that I use our own note, [[soc2-type-1-vs-type-2]], and the Wikipedia article [[system-and-organization-controls]].
