# One-On-One Notes Folder

Pointer to where my one-to-one notes live. They stay out of the vault.

## Source Location

A private folder per engineer in the company's document system, shared only between me and that engineer. Each engineer can read and edit their own folder. Anything that concerns performance, pay or health goes to the HR system, not even to that folder.

## Practice

- Every second week for each engineer, 30 minutes; weekly for the first three months after joining.
- Agenda is theirs first, mine second. Shared doc, written before the meeting.
- What reaches the vault: decisions that affect the team, written as decisions ([[what-i-delegated-in-2026]], [[team-working-agreements]]), never what someone said in confidence.
