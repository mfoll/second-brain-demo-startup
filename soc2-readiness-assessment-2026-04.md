# SOC 2 Readiness Assessment 2026-04

Ashcombe Assurance's written readiness assessment, delivered 2026-04-24 after the review of 2026-04-20 and 2026-04-21.

## Content

- 58 controls assessed against the draft [[soc2-control-matrix]]: 47 ready, 11 gaps.
- For each gap: the criterion affected, what evidence was missing, and a suggested remedy. Ashcombe is clear that remedies are suggestions; designing controls is our job.
- A note that the change management rule adopted on 2026-04-08 met the evidence standard if branch protection covered every production repository.

## How We Used It

Each gap became a ticket with an owner and a date before the 2026-07-31 as-of date. The list and outcomes are in [[soc2-readiness-review-2026-04-20]]. The two gaps still open in August became the two exceptions.
