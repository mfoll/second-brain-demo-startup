# Meridale Questionnaire Draft Answers 2025-10

Archived. The first draft of our answers to the [[meridale-security-questionnaire]], written 2025-09-22 to 2025-10-01 and superseded by the final version sent on 2025-10-10.

## Why It Is Kept

Two answers in this draft overstated tenant isolation: question 63 said tenant data was "logically isolated at the database level" and question 66 said cross-tenant access was "technically prevented". Both described intent, not mechanism. [[teodora-vasilescu]] flagged them before the call with Samir ([[meridale-security-review-call-2025-10-02]]), and the final answers described application-level filtering honestly.

That correction is the origin of the rule in [[answer-a-security-questionnaire]]: answer with the mechanism as it is today.
