# Dental Recall Reminders Request

Summary of an email thread with Luis Montoya, office manager at a three-practice dental group, between 2026-05-22 and 2026-07-14, about recall reminders.

## Summary

- Dental practices remind patients when a periodic checkup is due, months after the last visit. Today his staff track this in a spreadsheet and phone people.
- He wants Quaylane to send a recall message when a visit of a given type is due, with a booking link if online booking ever exists.
- He sent the recall intervals his practices use by category of visit, without any patient data.

## Why It Matters

Recall serves dental and physio, nearly half of ARR ([[clinic-segments-overview]]). It is one of the three themes in the [[roadmap-2027-draft]].

## Key Details

- 97 items tagged `recall-reminders` in the 2026 feedback log across 38 accounts.
- Recall needs a "due" concept per contact and appointment type that we do not store today.

## Caveats

One customer's thread, though a representative one. Recall touches what we store about people, so privacy counsel should review the data model before build.
