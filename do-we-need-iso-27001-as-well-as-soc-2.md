# Do We Need ISO 27001 As Well As SOC 2?

Some questionnaires ask for "SOC 2 or ISO 27001"; a few ask for ISO 27001 specifically. Would certification be worth it on top of SOC 2?

## Current Position

Deferred on 2026-09-28. Of the 9 questionnaires received in 2026, 8 accepted a SOC 2 report; one prospect asked for ISO 27001 and accepted the SOC 2 Type I plus a bridge letter. ISO 27001 certifies a management system, which overlaps heavily with what SOC 2 already makes us do, but the certification cycle and an extra auditor are a real cost for 14 people ([[iso-iec-27001]]).

## Revisit If

- Two or more enterprise prospects in the same quarter require ISO 27001 and will not accept SOC 2.
- A customer group outside our current market asks for it as a contract condition.
- After the first Type II report, when the control set is stable.
