# DevOps Research and Assessment

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-04-21.

## Summary

DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA) is a team, now part of Google Cloud, that surveys software engineers to research DevOps practices. Its work is the origin of the widely used delivery measures known as the DORA metrics.

## Why It Matters

Where the four measures in my monthly [[dora-metrics-log]] come from ([[dora-four-keys]]). Knowing they come from survey research across many organizations is a reminder that they describe delivery systems in general, and that our sample of five incidents a quarter is too small for fine comparisons.
