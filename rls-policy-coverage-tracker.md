# RLS Policy Coverage Tracker

Weekly report of which tenant-scoped tables have row-level security enabled, forced and covered by the `tenant_isolation` policy. Built by [[bram-verhoef]].

## Key Details

- Generated by a query over `pg_class` (`relrowsecurity`, `relforcerowsecurity`) joined to `pg_policies`, run every Monday and posted to the platform channel.
- Progress: 4 tables on 2026-06-26, 29 on 2026-07-13, 38 on 2026-08-28, 41 on 2026-10-19.
- Remaining: `report_daily_counts`, `report_room_utilization`, `waitlist_offers_archive`, `staff_room_links`, `audit_events`, `import_batches`.

## Caveats

"Covered" means the policy exists and is forced, not that it is correct. Correctness is the job of the pgTAP tests.

`quaylane-infra` repository, `tools/rls-coverage`; weekly output in the platform channel.
