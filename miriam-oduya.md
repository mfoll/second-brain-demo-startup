Executive coach. Independent; works mostly with founders and first-time executives in software companies. Introduced by [[celeste-arnaud]] in late 2025. Engaged by Quaylane for twelve monthly sessions in 2026 ([[coaching-agreement-miriam-oduya]]).

## How we work together

- One 90-minute video session a month, usually on a Monday or Tuesday. I send three lines beforehand on what has been hard; she asks for an example, not a description.
- I write a reflection within a week of each session. She does not read them unless I send them.
- Sessions are confidential. What goes into the vault is what I decided to do, not what was said about anyone else.
