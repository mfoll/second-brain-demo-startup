# Founders Circle 2026-09-10

Monthly founders' circle, six technical founders, two hours. I brought the platform hire question.

## What I took from it

- [[leon-brandauer|Leon Brandauer]] hired a first platform engineer at 22 people and thinks it was about a year late: two engineers burned out on the rotation first. His product is infrastructure-heavy, so I discount this somewhat.
- Two others had paid on-call stipends; both use a flat weekly amount, in the same range as our $300.
- One founder argued the opposite: a platform hire before product-market fit in the next segment is a luxury. Our next segment is enterprise clinic groups, where reliability is part of the product. I wrote that down as the strongest point for Kwabena's case.
