# Run An Engineering Interview Loop

How we hire an engineer, from opening the role to the offer call. Used for both [[engineering-hiring-2026]] roles; owned by me until the 2027 searches, when [[teodora-vasilescu]] takes it over.

## Inputs

- An approved headcount line in the budget.
- A role description reviewed by two engineers ([[backend-engineer-job-description-2026]] is the model).
- A scorecard with four to six criteria written before the first interview.

## Steps

1. **Open the role.** Post it; brief [[tess-okafor]] if we use her. Set the interview panel: three engineers plus Felix for product roles.
2. **Screen (30 minutes).** Recruiter or me. Questions from [[interview-question-bank]]. Be explicit about on-call and team size.
3. **Pairing session (90 minutes).** One engineer pairs with the candidate on a sanitized ticket. Never real customer data.
4. **Written design question (45 minutes, capped).** Read by two engineers independently.
5. **Team conversation (45 minutes).** Two engineers, focus on collaboration and disagreement.
6. **Scorecards before debrief.** Every interviewer submits a scorecard in the HR system before seeing anyone else's ([[interview-scorecards-folder]]).
7. **Debrief (45 minutes).** Go criterion by criterion. The hiring manager decides; dissent is recorded.
8. **Offer call** by the hiring manager within two working days. Call runner-ups personally.

## Guardrails

- Same questions per stage for every candidate in a search.
- No questions about personal circumstances. Nothing about candidates beyond the decision and criteria goes into the vault.
- Every declined finalist gets written feedback within a week.

## Verification

Time from application to offer: target under four weeks. The 2026 backend search took six; most of the delay was my calendar.
