# Coaching Reflection 2026-06

Written after the fifth session (2026-06-08), the week after we moved version 3 to September.

## What was hard

The slip ([[release-planning-2026-06-02]]). I had told Renata in March that the tenant migration would not touch the 3.0 date, and it took two engineers for 11 weeks. I did not want to be the person who says the date is wrong, so I said it three weeks later than I knew it.

## What Miriam asked

Who else on the team knew the date was wrong before I said it? Lucia and Teodora, both. Neither said so in a meeting. That is partly my doing: they had watched me defend the date.

## What I decided

- Ask for a written date-confidence check at each release planning: each feature lead writes high, medium or low, and why. Lucia runs it now as part of the release train.
- Hand the release train to Lucia in July, before 3.0, not after. Done on 2026-07-14.
- Tell Renata bad news on the day I know it. Not measurable, but she can call me on it.
