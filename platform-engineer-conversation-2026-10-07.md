# Platform Engineer Conversation 2026-10-07

One-hour call with [[rohan-deshpande|Rohan Deshpande]], a platform engineer introduced by [[sigrid-aaltonen]], to understand the role before writing a description. Not an interview.

## Points

- A first platform engineer in a small company should own three things from day one: alerting and on-call tooling, the deploy pipeline, and infrastructure as code. Owning only Terraform turns the role into a ticket queue.
- They should not be the only person on call. If product engineers leave the rotation entirely, they stop caring about what pages.
- Success in the first six months is measured in pages that stop happening and in how long a new engineer takes to deploy on day one.
- He asked what our out-of-hours page rate is. I said about 3 a week in Q3. He said that is manageable for six people and not for three.
