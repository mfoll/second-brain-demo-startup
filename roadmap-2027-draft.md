# Roadmap 2027 Draft

Draft v2 of the 2027 roadmap, written by Felix and me after the [[roadmap-offsite-2026-10-14]].

## Summary

Three themes with appetites, plus a fixed platform reservation:

| Theme | Appetite | Main items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient self-booking | 20 weeks | Booking widget, if go |
| Clinic group operations | 24 weeks | Reporting, cross-site room report, enterprise onboarding tooling |
| Recall and physio scheduling | 16 weeks | Recall reminders, group bookings |
| Platform reservation | 30% of capacity | Observability resumption, row-level security coverage, SOC 2 Type II, on-call |

## Why It Matters

It is the document the board will see on 2026-12-08, after cuts. It also shows both hiring variants, product engineer or platform engineer first.

## Key Details

- Capacity: about 46 engineer-weeks of roadmap work in 2027 with two hires from Q2; themes ask for 60.
- Only contractual dates appear; everything else is first or second half.

## Caveats

Over capacity by design until the cut on 2026-11-10. Do not quote it to customers.
