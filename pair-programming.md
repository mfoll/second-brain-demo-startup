# Pair programming

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-07.

## Summary

Pair programming is a development method in which two programmers work at one workstation. The driver writes code while the navigator reviews each line as it is written, and they switch roles often. The article covers claimed benefits, costs, variants such as remote pairing, and the research on how well pairs perform.

## Why It Matters

The practice at the center of [[hamish-pettigrew]]'s dissent on the [[code-review-policy]]: the navigator role is continuous review, which is his whole argument. It is also how we run the scheduling engine rotation ([[pairing-rotation-scheduling-engine]]) and the pairing stage of our interviews.
