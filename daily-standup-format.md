# Daily Standup Format

Retired on 2025-10-20 and replaced by [[written-async-updates]]. Kept for reference because new hires sometimes ask why we have no standup.

## The format as it was (2023-06 to 2025-10)

- Every working day at 09:30, 15 minutes planned, video call.
- Each engineer: yesterday, today, blockers. I went last and added company news.
- Problem-solving was supposed to happen after the call. In practice it happened inside it, and the call ran 20 to 25 minutes.

## Why it was retired

Raised at [[retro-2025-10-16]]: it split the morning for early starters, duplicated tickets and pull requests, and served me more than the engineers. The six-week trial of written updates was kept on 2025-12-04.
