# Meridale Pilot Report 2025-12

Six-month report on the three-clinic Meridale pilot, written by Imani and me for Victoria Ashdown, dated 2025-12-05.

## Summary

- Three clinics live since 2025-06-02, 47 admin users.
- Reminder coverage 94% of appointments; reported no-show rate at the pilot clinics went from 8.2% to 5.9% (clinic-reported baseline).
- Front-desk time on phone rebooking reduced, by the clinics' own estimate, by about 6 hours a week per clinic.

## Why It Matters

It was the evidence Victoria used to propose the 42-clinic contract to her board. It also lists the conditions her IT team would require, which became the contract conditions.

## Key Details

- Issues during the pilot: 61 tickets, none critical. One reminder delay in 2025-09.
- Requests: one view across sites, SSO, a waitlist that works without phone calls.

## Caveats

The no-show figures are the clinics' own and are not adjusted for season. We were careful to present them as reported, not as an effect we caused.
