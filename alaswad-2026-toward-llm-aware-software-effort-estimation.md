# Toward LLM-aware software effort estimation: a conceptual framework.

Alaswad F, Poovammal E, Aljaddouh B. Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence, 2026. Review article.

## Summary

A conceptual paper arguing that established estimation methods (COCOMO, function points, story points) assume human labor drives cost, and that this assumption breaks when language models write part of the code. Effort moves toward interaction management, validation, correction and integration. The authors argue the mismatch is structural rather than a matter of tuning parameters, illustrate it with exploratory observations, and propose a framework for estimation that accounts for assistant use.

## Why It Matters

Two uses for us. For [[engineering-metrics]], it is a reason not to trust any velocity number across the AI pilot boundary. For [[ai-assisted-development]], it names what our pilot reviewers felt: the work moved to validation ([[ai-assistant-pilot-results-2026-09]]).

## Caveats

Conceptual, with exploratory observations rather than a controlled study. We do not use story points for planning anyway, so the estimation proposals matter less to us than the diagnosis.
