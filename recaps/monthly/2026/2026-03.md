# March 2026

The month of the reminder outage. Everything else in March either led up to it or was reshaped by it.

## The tenant rollout and the outage

The [[full-tenant-database-rollout|full tenant rollout]] started at the beginning of the month after the pilot closed in February, and at the [[board-meeting-2026-03-10|board meeting on 10 March]] Margot asked what it would cost at 280 databases. A week later a migration batch exhausted the database connections and caused the [[reminder-outage-2026-03-17|reminder outage]]. Kwabena ran the incident; the rollout was paused the same day. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-03-17|17 March]].

## What we changed

The [[pingrove-call-2026-03-19|call with Pingrove]] settled how the gateway's rate limit works and what our workers must do about it. The [[postmortem-review-2026-03-24|blameless review]] agreed nine actions, tracked in [[reminder-outage-postmortem|the postmortem project]], and the queue-age alert was the first to land before the month was out. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-03-24|24 March]].

## Stepping out of incident roles

The outage was also a coaching topic. After the second session with Miriam I decided to stop taking incident roles and made Kwabena the default incident commander, announced at the review rather than arranged in private ([[coaching-reflection-2026-03|March reflection]]).

## The rest of the month

Ayesha started on 16 March and spent her first two weeks inside an incident and a postmortem, which she says was a fast way to learn the system. SOC 2 preparation with Ashcombe continued quietly in the background ([[soc2-type-1-audit|Type I audit]]).

## Carried into April

The rollout restart, gated on the migration throttle and reserved connections; PgBouncer; the on-call redesign; and the code review rule the auditor will need, set for the engineering meeting on 8 April.
