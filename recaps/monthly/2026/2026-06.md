# June 2026

Two decisions in ten days undid two plans from the start of the year, and the month still ended with the one date we could not miss.

## Version 3 moves to September

[[release-planning-2026-06-02|Release planning on 2 June]] moved 3.0 from June to September and split SSO out to ship alone. The cause was capacity spent on platform work, not the features ([[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip|lessons from the slip]]). See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-02|2 June]].

## The tenant isolation reversal

The [[architecture-review-2026-06-11|review on 11 June]] partly reversed the [[architecture-review-2025-11-18|November decision]]: dedicated databases for enterprise contracts only, row-level security for everyone else, and the 48 moved tenants folded back. The [[row-level-security-rollout|row-level security rollout]] and [[enterprise-dedicated-databases|enterprise dedicated databases]] started on 15 June. See [[recaps/daily/2026/2026-06-11|11 June]].

## The board

At the [[board-meeting-2026-06-16|board meeting on 16 June]] both decisions were accepted. Margot asked whether the first half of the year was wasted; my answer was that the migration tooling now serves Meridale's dedicated database. Renata was authorized to negotiate the extension with Copperwell.

## SSO for Meridale

[[sso-go-live-2026-06-30|SSO went live on 30 June]], a day before the contract date, with no incident. The one emergency change of the month was a SAML fix the day before, reviewed the next morning under the break-glass path. The first of Meridale's three conditions is met ([[meridale-commitments-tracker|commitments tracker]]).

## Carried into July

Bram starts on 6 July and takes over the row-level security work; Lucia takes over the release train; the fold-back waves begin mid-month.
