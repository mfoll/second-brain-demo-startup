# Thursday, June 11, 2026

## Partly reversing the database-per-tenant decision

The architecture review partly reversed the [[architecture-review-2025-11-18|November decision to give every tenant its own database]]. From today, dedicated databases are for enterprise contracts only, Meridale first, and every other tenant stays in, or returns to, the shared database under [[row-level-security|row-level security]]. The 48 tenants already moved will be folded back over the summer. Evidence, options and positions are in the [[architecture-review-2026-06-11|review that reversed it]], and the new decision record is [[adr-019-isolation-by-contract-tier|ADR 019]], which supersedes [[adr-014-database-per-tenant|ADR 014]].

## Why now

Migration times, the RDS bill and the March outage all pointed the same way, and Theo Mallory's review and the pen test said the shared schema was a sound base. Samir confirmed last week that Meridale needs a dedicated database for Meridale, not for everyone. That one email removed the last reason to continue.

## The room

Teodora agreed and asked that the move tooling be kept for enterprise tenants. Kwabena asked for coverage to be measured, not assumed. Hamish asked whether we were reversing because of the outage alone; my answer was no. The [[full-tenant-database-rollout|full rollout]] closes as Superseded, and two new projects start next week: the [[row-level-security-rollout|row-level security rollout]] and [[enterprise-dedicated-databases|enterprise dedicated databases]].

## For me

The pre-read did the work and the meeting took 70 minutes. I want reversals to be this cheap and this public ([[reversals-should-be-cheap-and-public|my note on reversals]]).
