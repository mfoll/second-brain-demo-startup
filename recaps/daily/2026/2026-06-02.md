# Tuesday, June 2, 2026

## Version 3 moves to September

Release planning moved Quaylane 3.0 from late June to mid-September and split SSO out to ship on its own before Meridale's contractual date. Shipping in June with calendars flagged off, or without them, was considered and rejected; the options and decisions are in the [[release-planning-2026-06-02|planning note]], and the project state in [[version-3-release|version 3]].

## Why it slipped

Not the features. Two engineers spent most of the spring on the tenant migration and the outage clean-up, and I kept the June date anyway. I wrote that down in [[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip|lessons from the slip]] while it was still uncomfortable.
