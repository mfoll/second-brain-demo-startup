# Wednesday, April 8, 2026

## One recorded approval per pull request

The Wednesday engineering meeting adopted one recorded approval on every pull request to main, enforced by branch protection from the end of the month, with a break-glass path for emergencies. Two approvals, risky-paths-only and review-after-merge were all discussed and dropped; the [[engineering-meeting-2026-04-08|meeting note]] has why. I owe the team the written [[code-review-policy-document|policy]] and the [[ship-an-emergency-change|emergency change procedure]].

## Hamish's dissent

Hamish argued that pair-programmed commits have had more review than a skimmed pull request and should count. Patrice, joining from Ashcombe, said only a recorded approval is evidence for the auditor. I decided for the rule without the pairing exception and said I think Hamish is right about quality. He asked for his position to be recorded, and it is, in [[should-pair-programmed-commits-count-as-reviewed|the open question on pairing]].

## Afterwards

Hamish and Min-jae will keep pairing and add a third engineer as approver. Kwabena takes the branch protection change through Terraform.
