# Tuesday, November 18, 2025

## A database for every tenant

The architecture review chose to move every tenant out of the shared schema into its own PostgreSQL database, on Teodora's [[tenant-isolation-design-doc|design document]]. The trigger was the isolation block of [[meridale-security-questionnaire|Meridale's questionnaire]], where our honest answer had been "a column and careful code". Row-level security and a schema per tenant were discussed and set aside; the reasons are in the [[architecture-review-2025-11-18|review note]] and the decision record [[adr-014-database-per-tenant|ADR 014]].

## Kwabena's warning

Kwabena put two concerns on record: connection counts growing with every tenant database, and migrations fanning out across hundreds of databases. We agreed to handle both during the rollout. I am not fully comfortable that neither is in the pilot's success criteria, and said so to Teodora after the meeting; we left it there for now.

## What follows

I opened [[tenant-isolation-migration|the migration project]] and planned a pilot for 12 small tenants from January. Renata will let Dana tell Meridale that dedicated databases are on the roadmap.
