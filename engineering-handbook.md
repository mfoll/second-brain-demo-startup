# Engineering Handbook

The team's internal wiki: how we build, review, deploy and operate Quaylane. About 60 pages, owned by me, edited by everyone.

## Key Sections

- `Policies`: code review, AI tools (draft), access requests. Policy pages are short and change only by decision.
- `How we work`: working agreements, retros, written updates.
- `Guides`: local setup, deploys, feature flags, how to review well (the advice that is deliberately not in the policy).
- `On call`: the handbook and quarterly reviews.
- `People`: career ladder draft, onboarding plan.

## Why It Matters

The vault records decisions and context; the handbook is the operating manual the team actually reads. When they disagree, the handbook is updated and the vault links to the decision.

## Caveats

Pages under `Guides` go stale; each has an owner and a "last checked" date, and about a fifth were overdue on 2026-10-20.
