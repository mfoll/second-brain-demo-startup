# How students use generative AI for software testing: An observational study.

Ardic B, Le Dilavrec Q, Zaidman A. Empirical Software Engineering, 2026.

## Summary

An observational study of 12 undergraduate students with foundational testing knowledge, writing unit tests with ChatGPT (GPT-3.5). The authors identify four interaction strategies, depending on whether the test idea and the test implementation came from the assistant or the student, and distinguish one-shot from iterative prompting styles. They discuss concerns about control, output quality and learning for novices.

## Why It Matters

Read after [[olu-ajayi]]'s internship, where tests were exactly what he used the assistant for. The distinction between "the idea came from the tool" and "the implementation came from the tool" is a useful question to put in the [[ai-coding-tools-policy-draft]] for junior engineers and interns: we want the test idea to be theirs.

## Caveats

Twelve students and an older model. Not about professional teams.
