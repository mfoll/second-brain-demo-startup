# Roadmap 2027

The 2027 product roadmap, written with Felix Ostrowski and Renata Castellano and presented at the board meeting of 2026-12-08. It has to fit the headcount in [[engineering-budget-2027]] and leave room for the SOC 2 Type II work.

## Outcome

A 2027 roadmap agreed with the CEO and head of product and presented at the 2026-12-08 board meeting.

## Current State

- Draft v2 in [[roadmap-2027-draft]] after the [[roadmap-offsite-2026-10-14]]. Three themes: patient self-booking ([[online-booking-widget]]), clinic group operations (reporting, cross-site rooms, a second enterprise account), and recall reminders for dental and physio.
- Capacity assumption: seven engineers plus two hires from 2027-Q2, about 60% of time on roadmap work, the rest on platform, on-call, SOC 2 and support.
- The first hire is still open. Felix wants a product engineer; Kwabena wants a platform engineer. The roadmap shows both variants. See [[should-we-hire-a-platform-engineer-or-keep-everyone-on-call]].
- Draft v2 is about 30% over capacity. Something has to go; see [[what-should-the-2027-roadmap-cut]].

## Scope and Boundaries

Product features and the engineering work that enables them. Platform items (observability resumption in 2027-Q1, finishing row-level security coverage) appear as capacity reservations, owned under [[run-the-platform]]. The budget itself belongs to [[engineering-budget-2027]].

## Working Context

Felix and I write the roadmap as themes with appetites, not dated feature lists; see [[appetite-based-scoping]]. After the [[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip|version 3 slip]] we agreed that only contractual dates appear on the customer-facing roadmap.

Inputs: the top themes from the [[customer-feedback-log]], the [[booking-widget-discovery-interviews]], the [[churn-analysis-2025]], Meridale's asks from the October business review, and what Renata told Copperwell during the [[seed-extension-diligence]].
