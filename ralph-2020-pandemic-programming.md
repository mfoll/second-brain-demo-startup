# Pandemic programming: How COVID-19 affects software developers and how their organizations can help.

Ralph P, Baltes S, Adisaputri G, et al. Empirical Software Engineering, 2020.

## Summary

A questionnaire survey of 2,225 developers in 53 countries, built mainly from validated scales and analyzed with structural equation modeling. The pandemic hurt developers' well-being and productivity; well-being and productivity were closely related; disaster preparedness, fear and home-office ergonomics affected both. Exploratory results suggest some groups were affected more than others and that different people need different kinds of support. The authors recommend focusing on well-being and home-office ergonomics.

## Why It Matters

Read after the [[team-health-survey-2025-11]] came back with a 3.0 on workload. The useful idea was the link between well-being and productivity as one thing rather than a trade-off. It is behind the home-office equipment allowance Renata approved in 2026-01, and behind asking about workload in every survey rather than only about output.

## Caveats

Pandemic conditions, early 2020. Our team chose remote work; the shock component does not apply.
