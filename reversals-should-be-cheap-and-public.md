# Reversals Should Be Cheap And Public

## Stance

When evidence turns against a decision, reverse it early, in a meeting with the numbers in front of everyone, and write it down the same week. Letting a project drift costs more than admitting the call was wrong.

## Why I Think This

- [[sigrid-aaltonen]] said it before the June review and the review proved it: 70 minutes, a clear pre-read, no blame, decision made.
- I waited about six weeks longer than I should have. By late April the cost and migration numbers were already clear; I wanted Theo's report as cover.

## What Would Change My Mind

A team that reads frequent reversals as indecision. So far the opposite: [[teodora-vasilescu]] said the reversal made her more willing to propose bold designs, not less.

## Implications

Every big decision record gets a "review trigger" line, as [[adr-019-isolation-by-contract-tier]] does.
