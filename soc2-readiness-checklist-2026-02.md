# SOC 2 Readiness Checklist 2026-02

Archived. My first attempt, in 2026-01 and early 2026-02, at listing what SOC 2 would need: 34 checklist lines grouped by "policies", "technical", "people", built from the 17 "No" answers in the [[meridale-security-questionnaire]] and a few articles. Superseded on 2026-02-17 by the [[soc2-control-matrix]], after the kickoff with Ashcombe ([[soc2-kickoff-with-ashcombe-2026-02-10]]).

## Why It Was Replaced

- It listed tasks, not controls: "set up branch protection" rather than "every change has a recorded approval, evidenced by X".
- It had no owners, frequencies or evidence locations, so it could not tell us what to do every quarter.
- It missed whole areas the criteria require: risk assessment, vendor management, board oversight.

## Worth Keeping

The mapping from questionnaire answers to tasks is still a fair picture of where we started in early 2026. Nothing here is current; use the control matrix.
