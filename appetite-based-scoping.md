# Appetite-Based Scoping

Decide how much time a problem is worth before deciding the solution, then shape the solution to fit. Felix and I adopted it for the 2027 roadmap after the version 3 slip.

## How we use it

- Each roadmap theme gets an appetite in engineer-weeks, for example 16 weeks for recall and physio scheduling.
- The team shapes a solution that fits the appetite. If it cannot, the theme is reshaped or dropped; the appetite does not grow quietly.
- Contractual items are the exception: they get a date and as much capacity as they need, as SSO did in 2026.

## Why we switched

The [[version-3-release-plan]] fixed both scope and date, which left capacity as the only variable. When two engineers went to the tenant migration, the plan had nowhere to bend except the date. See [[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip]].

## Limits

It does not help with work whose size is unknown, like the tenant migration. Platform work stays a capacity reservation instead.
