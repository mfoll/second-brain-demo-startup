# Churn Analysis 2025

Imani Brooks and I reviewed every account that left in 2025, using the billing system, exit calls and the [[customer-feedback-log]]. Finished 2026-01-20.

## Summary

- 23 accounts churned in 2025 out of about 260 at the start of the year; 21 were single-site.
- Reasons: closed or acquired 8, price 5, moved to a system with online booking 4, reminder problems 2, unknown 4.
- Gross revenue churn was about 7% for the year; logo churn about 9%.

## Why It Matters

It is the evidence behind [[what-drives-churn-among-small-clinics]] and part of the case for the [[online-booking-widget]]. It also led Renata to raise the included SMS volume on the smallest plan in 2025-10.

## Key Details

- Exit call held for 12 of 23 accounts, including [[ezra-feldman-churn-call-2025-08-14]].
- Median tenure of churned accounts: 13 months.
- Family practice churned at twice the rate of dental.

## Caveats

Reasons are what people told us. "Price" and "feature" overlap. Definitions follow the usual logo and revenue churn distinction ([[churn-rate]]).
