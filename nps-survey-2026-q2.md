# NPS Survey 2026-Q2

Customer success's in-app NPS survey to all admin users, run from 2026-06-08 to 2026-06-26.

## Summary

- 612 responses from 1,840 admin users invited (33%), across 214 accounts.
- NPS 41, down from 47 in 2025-Q4. Promoters 55%, detractors 14%.
- Most common detractor themes: reporting (31 comments), the March outage (19), reminders cost (12).

## Why It Matters

The drop is mostly the outage; detractors who mentioned it were concentrated in the 61 affected clinics. Reporting as the top detractor theme supports the clinic group operations theme in the [[roadmap-2027-draft]].

## Key Details

- Front-desk users score higher (NPS 48) than practice managers (NPS 29). Managers want numbers we do not give them.
- Comments go into the feedback log with the `nps` channel.

## Caveats

Response rate is decent but self-selected. A Q4 survey will show whether the outage effect has faded.
