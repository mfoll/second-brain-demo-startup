# Venture Round

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-06-17.

## Summary

A venture round is a funding round used in venture capital financing, through which start-ups raise investment, generally from venture capital firms and other institutional investors. The article covers the sequence of rounds from seed onward.

## Why It Matters

I read it in June, a few weeks after the [[seed-extension-kickoff-2026-05-04]], because I had never been on the company side of a round. At my previous employer funding was something that happened far above me. It gave me the vocabulary for Renata's plan: an extension of the seed rather than a new priced round, led by the existing lead investor, with a new investor joining. Renata and [[gwen-talbot]] own the details; I needed enough to understand why diligence happens and what it is for.
