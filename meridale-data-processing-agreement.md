# Meridale Data Processing Agreement

Summary of the DPA signed with Meridale Clinic Group alongside the main contract, negotiated by [[rania-saleh]] and reviewed by [[abigail-ferris]] in 2025-12.

## Differences From Our Template

- Breach notification within 48 hours of confirmation, instead of "without undue delay".
- A dedicated database with its own encryption key for Meridale's data ([[should-meridale-get-a-customer-managed-encryption-key]]).
- Annual right to request our SOC 2 report and pen test summary.
- Deletion certificate within 30 days of contract end.
- 30 days' notice for subprocessor changes, as in the template.

## Engineering Obligations Tracked

The 48-hour notice is in the [[security-incident-response-plan]] customer table. The dedicated database went live 2026-08-21.
