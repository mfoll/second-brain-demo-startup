# Mentor Lunch 2025-05-22

Lunch with [[celeste-arnaud|Celeste Arnaud]], my former manager, two years into Quaylane.

## What she said

- I described a typical week. She counted that I was the reviewer on more than half of the pull requests and the person paged for most incidents. Her line: the team will cap at my throughput.
- First-time founders who come from engineering management often try to be both the strongest engineer and the manager. One of those jobs has to go first, and it is usually the one you like more.
- Do not copy her old company's process. Copy the reason behind each piece and check whether the reason exists yet.

## What I did

Nothing immediately, which I note honestly. The idea of a coach came back in late 2025, and she introduced me to [[miriam-oduya]]. This lunch is the start of [[grow-as-a-leader]] as an area of its own.
