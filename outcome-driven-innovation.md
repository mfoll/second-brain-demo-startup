# Outcome-driven innovation

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-06-13.

## Summary

Describes outcome-driven innovation, a strategy and innovation process built on the idea that people buy products and services to get jobs done, and that each job has measurable outcomes the customer is trying to achieve. The method ties a company's product decisions to those customer-defined measures rather than to feature requests.

## Why It Matters

Felix introduced the jobs-to-be-done vocabulary to the team in 2025 and this article is the formal version he pointed me to. Our own list is much lighter: six jobs, no outcome statements, no surveys. See [[front-desk-jobs-to-be-done]]. The useful idea for us is the measure: "refill a cancelled slot before it is wasted" became "minutes from cancellation to refill", which is how we judged the [[waitlist-autofill]] beta.

## Caveats

A formal, survey-heavy method designed for larger companies. We borrow the framing, not the process.
