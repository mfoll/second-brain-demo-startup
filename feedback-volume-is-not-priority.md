# Feedback Volume Is Not Priority

## Stance

The number of times a request appears in the feedback log tells us how often it is said, not how much it matters. We rank by accounts affected, by the job it serves and by what it costs, and we read the count last.

## Scope

Applies to the monthly product review and the roadmap. It does not apply to bugs, where frequency is a fair proxy for impact.

## Why I think this

- In 2025 one account logged 31 items about a print layout. It was never in the top ten by accounts.
- Reminder delivery items spiked in 2026-03 and 2026-04 after the outage and stopped once the fixes shipped. A raw count would have put reminders on the 2027 roadmap.
- Online booking leads by both items and accounts, which is why I take it seriously despite my doubts in [[booking-widget-is-a-different-product]].

## What would change my mind

If we found that high-volume requests predict churn better than account counts do. The 2026 churn redo in [[what-drives-churn-among-small-clinics]] could show that.

## Implications

The [[customer-feedback-log]] summary for each review shows accounts first, items second.
