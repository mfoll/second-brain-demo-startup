# Incident Commander Should Not Be The Founder

## Stance

I should not hold an incident role. The founder's job during an incident is to protect the commander's focus, take the CEO and customer executives off their hands, and make commitments only the company can make.

## Why I Think This

- On 2026-03-17 I was scribe while answering Renata and Meridale. The timeline has a 50-minute gap because of it ([[postmortem-review-2026-03-24]]).
- People defer to the founder in the room. Kwabena runs a calmer incident when I am not in the channel.
- It was the first concrete delegation from my coaching with [[miriam-oduya]], and the easiest to keep.

## What Would Change My Mind

An incident where the commander needs a decision only I can make and cannot reach me. Escalation by phone covers that so far.

## Implications

[[run-an-incident]] says it explicitly. I join an incident channel only when the commander asks.
