# Postmortem documentation

## Summary

Wikipedia article on project post-mortems: a process to identify the causes of a project failure and how to prevent them, distinguished from a retrospective, which reviews both positive and negative aspects.

## Why It Matters

I read it the night before [[postmortem-review-2026-03-24]] looking for a format. It was less useful than I hoped: it is about project failures, not production incidents. The useful line was the distinction from a retrospective, which is why our review spent its first ten minutes on what went right during the incident too.

## Caveats

Not about incident postmortems or blameless practice specifically. Last edited 2025-10-03 at re-check.
