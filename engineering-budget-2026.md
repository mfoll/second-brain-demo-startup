# Engineering Budget 2026

The 2026 engineering budget as approved by the board in 2025-12, with actuals and forecast through year end as of 2026-10-05.

## What Went to Plan

- Headcount: two hires planned and made ([[engineering-hiring-2026]]).
- Tooling stayed within 5% of plan.

## What Did Not

- AWS: planned $38,000, forecast $44,000. The per-tenant database rollout added up to $3,100 a month on RDS from 2026-03 to 2026-08.
- Audit and security: planned $0 in 2025-12, forecast $38,500. SOC 2 was added in 2026-03 after the Meridale contract ([[board-meeting-2026-03-10]]).
- Consulting: $9,000 unplanned for [[theo-mallory]]'s PostgreSQL review in 2026-05.

## Lesson for 2027

Budget compliance as a recurring line and price architecture decisions before the board sees them.
