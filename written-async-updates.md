# Written Async Updates

Our replacement for the daily standup since 2025-10-20: each engineer posts three lines in `#eng-updates` by 10:30 on working days (done, doing, blocked on), and the team meets live only on Tuesday and Thursday for 30 minutes.

## Why it works for us

- Most of the team is remote and starts at different times.
- Writing forces a blocker to be named; talking lets it be mentioned and forgotten.
- It leaves a searchable record, which helped more than once in postmortems.

## Rules that make it work

- Three lines, not a diary.
- A blocker names a person or a thread.
- Skip on a focus day if you said so the day before.
- Anything that needs discussion goes to the next sync or a thread, not into the update.
