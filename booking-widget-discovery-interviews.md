# Booking Widget Discovery Interviews

Felix and Mai's summary of twelve interviews held between 2026-08-17 and 2026-09-18 with seven practice managers and five front-desk leads from current customers.

## Summary

- Practice managers want online booking to cut phone volume; 8 of the 12 said phone booking takes more front-desk time than anything else.
- Front-desk leads accept it only with control: which appointment types and which hours are bookable online, and the ability to hold slots back.
- Nobody wanted patient accounts. A booking link and a confirmation message are enough.

## Why It Matters

It reframed the widget from a patient feature to a front-desk feature, which shifted my opinion in [[booking-widget-is-a-different-product]]. Joanne Pruett's condition (front desk can hold slots back) and Ngaire Tamihana's enthusiasm are both recorded here.

## Key Details

- 9 of 12 would turn it on for returning patients; 5 of 12 for new patients.
- Most requested control: limit online booking to a few appointment types.
- Most feared outcome: people booking the wrong type and taking a long slot.

## Caveats

Current customers only, all chosen by customer success. No churned clinics except a short follow-up email from Ezra Feldman.
