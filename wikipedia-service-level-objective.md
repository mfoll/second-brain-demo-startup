# Service-level objective

## Summary

Wikipedia article defining an SLO as a target value or range for a service level measured by a service level indicator (SLI), commonly part of a service-level agreement, and also used internally by organizations practicing SRE.

## Why It Matters

Meridale's contract has an availability clause but nothing on reminder delivery, and [[hana-kovarik]] asked what we promise on reminders. The SLI and SLO split gave us the shape of an answer: the indicator is "share of reminders sent within 15 minutes of schedule", the objective is the number we argue about in [[what-is-our-reminder-delivery-target]].
