# A Founder Should Not Stay Incident Commander

## Stance

Once a company has someone who knows the system well and is calm under pressure, the founder who built it should stop commanding incidents. Be a resource the commander can call, not the commander.

## Scope

Day-to-day incidents. In a severity 1 incident with contractual or regulatory consequences I still join, as communications support to Renata, not as commander.

## Why I Think This

- In 2025 I commanded every incident. I also debugged in all of them, which meant nobody was coordinating while I had my head in logs.
- On 2026-03-17 [[kwabena-asare]] was incident commander for the [[reminder-outage-2026-03-17]]. The coordination was better than in any incident I had run, and I was free to work the problem.
- Others stop learning the system under pressure when the founder always takes over.

## What Would Change My Mind

An incident where the commander's lack of context clearly made things worse and my presence would have shortened it. Has not happened since.

## Implications

Kwabena is default commander during working hours since [[postmortem-review-2026-03-24]]; the role is defined in [[run-an-incident]]. I stay off the incident channel for the first 15 minutes unless paged.
