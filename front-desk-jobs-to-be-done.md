# Front-Desk Jobs To Be Done

Felix's list of the jobs a front-desk coordinator hires Quaylane for, written in 2025-04 and revised after the 2026 interviews. We use it to test whether a request is a new job or a better way to do an existing one.

## The jobs

1. Fill today's book without double-booking a room or a person.
2. Get people to turn up (reminders).
3. Refill a cancelled slot before it is wasted (waitlist).
4. Answer "when can I come in?" on the phone in under a minute.
5. Rearrange the day when a staff member is out.
6. Tell the practice manager how the week went.

## How we use it

- [[online-booking-widget]] serves job 4 by removing the phone call; it is a new way to do an existing job, not a new job. That framing helped Felix argue that front-desk staff are users of it too.
- Reporting requests are job 6, which we serve badly. That is why reporting sits in the clinic group operations theme of the [[roadmap-2027-draft]].

The framework comes from the jobs-to-be-done school of product thinking; see [[outcome-driven-innovation]] for one formal version.
