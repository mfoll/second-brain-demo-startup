# A Review Of Organization-Oriented Phishing Research

Althobaiti K, Alsufyani N. PeerJ Computer Science, 2024.

## Summary

A systematic review of 163 research articles on phishing aimed at organizations. The authors group the work and find that most studies focus on detecting phishing and on testing users' awareness, while other layers of protection, such as mitigating the effects of a successful phish, get less attention.

## Why It Matters

Our [[security-roadmap-2027]] proposes a simulated phishing exercise. This review is a useful reminder that awareness testing is only one layer; for a team of 14 the more valuable controls are the ones that limit damage when someone clicks: SSO with MFA everywhere, no shared credentials, and a clear way to report a suspicious message without embarrassment. Customer success is the most exposed group, since they receive email from hundreds of clinics.

## Caveats

A literature map, not evidence on which interventions work best. Read the original for its categories before citing specifics.
