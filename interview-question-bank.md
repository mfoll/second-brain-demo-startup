# Interview Question Bank

Questions we use in the engineering loop, grouped by stage. Each interviewer picks from the list for their stage so that candidates for the same role get comparable questions ([[structured-interview]]).

## Recruiter or hiring manager screen

- Tell me about a production problem you were on the hook for. What did you do in the first hour?
- What does a good code review from a colleague look like to you?

## Pairing session (90 minutes)

- A sanitized `quaylane-api` ticket: double-booking across time zones. We look for how the candidate reads unfamiliar code, not whether they finish.
- Platform variant: a Terraform change that would recreate a database. Spot it, then make it safe.

## Written design question (take-home, 45 minutes, unpaid, capped)

- Design the job that offers a cancelled slot to waitlisted patients. What can go wrong with SMS delivery?

## Team conversation

- Describe a decision you disagreed with and still followed.
- How do you want to hear that your work is not good enough?

## Retired

- Whiteboard algorithm questions (dropped 2025-03; told us little about the work).
- The paid take-home project (dropped 2026-01 after five candidates; too slow, and it favored people with free evenings).
