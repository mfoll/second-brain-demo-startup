# Observability (software)

## Summary

Wikipedia article describing observability as the ability to collect data about a program's execution, internal state and communication between components, using logging and tracing, and as the first step in triaging an outage. One stated goal is to reduce the prior knowledge needed to debug.

## Why It Matters

The "less prior knowledge" goal is the whole case for the [[observability-overhaul]]. Today only [[kwabena-asare]], [[teodora-vasilescu]] and I can diagnose the reminder pipeline from logs. On 2026-03-17 that knowledge was spread across three people in three channels.
