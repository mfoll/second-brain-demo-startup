# Waitlist Autofill Beta Metrics

Dashboard panel and final summary of the waitlist autofill beta, 2026-05-11 to 2026-08-21, 14 clinics.

## Summary

- 1,140 offers sent; 696 accepted within the 30-minute hold (61%); 302 expired; 142 declined.
- Median time from cancellation to refilled slot: 22 minutes.
- Front-desk confirmation median: 4 minutes after acceptance.

## Why It Matters

These numbers closed the matching argument and went into the 3.0 launch material. They also showed that offers at night were a problem (acceptance 23% for offers sent after 20:00), which led to the send window.

## Key Details

- Acceptance by segment: physio 66%, dental 58%, family practice 55%.
- Slots refilled per clinic per week: 5.6 on average.

## Caveats

Beta clinics volunteered and are more engaged than average. Adoption after GA is lower per clinic (3.9 refills a week in the first month).
