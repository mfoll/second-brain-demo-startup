# Churn rate

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-10.

## Summary

Defines churn rate as the share of customers or members who leave a defined group over a period, and describes its use in judging retention and the stability of subscription and contract revenue.

## Why It Matters

Imani and I had been using "churn" for three different numbers. After reading this we fixed two definitions for Quaylane: logo churn (accounts that leave in a period, over accounts at the start) and gross revenue churn (lost recurring revenue over starting recurring revenue). The [[churn-analysis-2025]] and [[what-drives-churn-among-small-clinics]] use those definitions.

## Caveats

Definitions only. Our small-clinic numbers are too few per month to read trends without smoothing over a quarter.
