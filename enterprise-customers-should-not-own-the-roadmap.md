# Enterprise Customers Should Not Own The Roadmap

## Stance

One enterprise customer should get contractual commitments, not a seat at the roadmap. Their requests compete in the same review as everyone else's, weighted by revenue but not decided by it.

## Scope

Feature roadmap. Contractual security, availability and isolation commitments are a different category and get done.

## Why I think this

- In 2026 Meridale's three conditions (SSO, dedicated database, SOC 2 Type I) took a large share of engineering capacity. They were the right conditions to accept, and they also crowded out almost everything the other 279 accounts asked for.
- Their next asks (cross-site room report, email waitlist offers) are reasonable. If we accept each one as a commitment, the roadmap becomes Meridale's.
- At 17% of ARR, losing them would hurt. Building only for them would hurt longer.

## What would change my mind

A second and third enterprise account asking for the same things. Then those requests are a segment, not a customer.

## Implications

Meridale's asks go into the [[customer-feedback-log]] like anyone's. Victoria Ashdown hears the outcome at the business review, with reasons.
