# Levels Of Delegation

A scale for saying exactly how much of a decision I hand over. [[miriam-oduya]] introduced it in the first coaching session ([[coaching-session-2026-02-09]]). Versions of it are common in management writing; this is the five-level form we use.

1. **Look into it and tell me.** I decide.
2. **Recommend.** You propose, I decide.
3. **Decide, but check with me first.** You decide unless I object.
4. **Decide and tell me.** I hear about it afterwards.
5. **Decide.** I do not need to hear about it.

## Why it matters

Most of my delegation failures in 2025 were level confusion: I meant 2 and the other person heard 4, or the reverse. Saying the number out loud removed most of that.

## Current uses

- Release train: Lucia at 4 ([[release-train-handover-2026-07-14]]).
- Incident command: Kwabena at 5 during working hours.
- Interview loop for 2027: Teodora at 3, moving to 4 after the first search.
