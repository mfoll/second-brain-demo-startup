# Should V3 Ship Without SSO?

> [!warning]
> Superseded on 2026-06-02 by the decision recorded in [[release-planning-2026-06-02]]: SSO shipped alone on 2026-06-30 and 3.0 moved to 2026-09-15. Kept for the reasoning trail.

## Question (as asked on 2026-05-12)

If multi-location calendars cannot make 2026-06-23, should 3.0 ship on time with waitlist autofill and calendars, and SSO follow as 3.1 just before Meridale's 2026-07-01 date?

## Why it was asked

In early May I thought SSO was the risk and calendars were safe. I had it the wrong way round: by the end of May SSO was nearly done and calendars were five weeks behind.

## What replaced it

The real question became which feature to split out, and the answer was SSO, because it was the only one with a contractual date. See [[enterprise-sso]] and [[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip]].
