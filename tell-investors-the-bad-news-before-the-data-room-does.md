# Tell Investors The Bad News Before The Data Room Does

## Stance

In diligence and board updates, engineering setbacks (an outage, a reversed decision, a slipped release, an audit exception) should be stated first, with numbers and the trigger, before the reader finds them in a document.

## Why I Think This

- At [[board-meeting-2026-06-16]] I led with the outage and the reversal. Margot's questions were about cost and process, not about whether we were hiding something.
- In the [[technical-diligence-call-2026-08-19]] I raised the reversal before Arun did, with the 48 tenants, the 2 h 40 min migrations and the $3,100 a month. It took ten minutes. The point I had not prepared, the bus factor, took thirty.
- Renata shared the SOC 2 exceptions with Meridale before they asked, and Samir's response was about dates, not trust.

## Scope

Facts the team can share. Confidential financial terms stay with Renata.

## What Would Change My Mind

A case where early disclosure of an engineering problem visibly cost us a deal or a term, without the problem coming out later anyway.
