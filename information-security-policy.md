# Information Security Policy

Quaylane's information security policy, approved by Renata and me on 2026-05-12. Eleven pages, written in plain language because every employee signs it at onboarding.

## Sections

Purpose and scope; roles (Nikolas owns the program, Colette owns people and vendor controls); acceptable use; access control (SSO with MFA, least privilege, quarterly review); change management (one recorded approval, break-glass path); data classification (public, internal, confidential, customer data); encryption; device security (disk encryption, MDM since 2026-09); incident response; vendor management; exceptions process.

## Notes

- Customer data is one class, with the rule that it never leaves production systems except through approved exports to the customer themselves.
- The policy is the anchor several controls point to; any change goes through the annual review in 2027-05 unless an auditor-agreed fix needs it earlier.
- Acknowledgements: 14 of 14 staff signed by 2026-06-15.
