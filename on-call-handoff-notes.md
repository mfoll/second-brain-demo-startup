# On-Call Handoff Notes

The Monday handoff notes posted in `#oncall` since 2026-04-20: about 26 notes by 2026-10-19.

## Key Details

- Format borrowed from [[leon-brandauer]]'s team: pages received, anything still open, alerts changed, what to watch. Five lines.
- The best source for why a page happened; PagerDuty only says that it did.
- Most frequent "what to watch" item in Q3: Pingrove delivery slowdowns on Monday mornings when clinics send the week's reminders.

## Why It Matters

Input to the quarterly paging review and to [[how-many-pages-a-week-is-too-many]]. Also the place to see whether a demoted alert came back.

## Source Location

Slack channel `#oncall`, exported quarterly to `On-call / handoffs` in the engineering shared drive.
