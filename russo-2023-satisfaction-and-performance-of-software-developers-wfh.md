# Satisfaction and performance of software developers during enforced work from home in the COVID-19 pandemic.

Russo D, Hanel PHP, Altnickel S, van Berkel N. Empirical Software Engineering, 2023.

## Summary

Two studies of software engineers working from home (an exploratory longitudinal study with 192 participants and a confirmatory study with 290), built on self-determination theory. Time spent on activities such as coding, bug fixing and helping others was similar to pre-pandemic work and unrelated to general well-being. Activity-specific factors, especially autonomy during the activity, predicted satisfaction and productivity. Satisfaction and autonomy were higher when helping others and lower when bug fixing. The authors suggest policies that support autonomy, relatedness and competence.
