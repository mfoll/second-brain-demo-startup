# ISO/IEC 27001

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-09.

## Summary

ISO/IEC 27001 sets requirements for establishing, running, maintaining and continually improving an information security management system, including how to assess and treat risks, for organizations of any type and size. Certification by an independent body is voluntary unless a law, contract or other scheme requires it, and it covers only the scope named on the certificate. First published in 2005; the current edition is from 2022, amended in 2024.

## Why It Matters

It is the reference for [[do-we-need-iso-27001-as-well-as-soc-2]]. The useful distinction for us: ISO 27001 certifies a management system and its scope, while a SOC 2 report gives an auditor's opinion on specific controls. Customers who accept either are asking for the same comfort. The point that certification is only required when a contract demands it is why the question is Deferred, not Open: no customer contract demands it yet.
