# Leon Brandauer

CTO and co-founder of another start-up, a logistics software company of about 30 people. A peer in the founders' circle: six technical founders who meet once a month for two hours, with a rule that nothing said leaves the room except what each person chooses to note about their own company.

## Why he matters

That is one input to [[should-we-hire-a-platform-engineer-or-keep-everyone-on-call]], weighed against the fact that their product is infrastructure-heavy and ours is not.
- He shares his on-call handbook freely; the handoff note format in our [[on-call-handbook]] came from his.
