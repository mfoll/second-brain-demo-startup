# Co-Founder Split With Renata

Who does what between [[renata-castellano]] and me. First written 2024-02, rewritten 2026-02 after a month of us both answering Meridale on the same questions, and reviewed again 2026-09.

## Renata

Company strategy, fundraising and investor relations, the board, sales and customer success leadership, company budget and finance (with [[gwen-talbot]] and [[colette-fairbanks]]), legal and contracts, hiring outside engineering and product.

## Me

Engineering team and hiring, architecture and platform, reliability, security and compliance, the engineering budget, technical diligence, the engineering page of every board deck.

## Shared

- Product direction with [[felix-ostrowski]]: Renata owns the "why", Felix the "what", I own the "how and when".
- Enterprise deals: Renata and Dana lead; I join for security and architecture.
- Culture and company-wide rituals.

## Rules We Keep

- One voice to the team and the board after a disagreement.
- Either of us can veto a customer commitment that touches the other's area. The Meridale conditions passed both.
- If both of us are needed in a meeting, one of us is probably not.

## What Changed in 2026

I stopped attending pitch meetings and sales demos unless security is on the agenda. Renata stopped forwarding customer feature requests to engineers directly; they go through the [[customer-feedback-log]].
