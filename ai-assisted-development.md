# AI-Assisted Development

Exploration cluster on coding assistants and agents in our own engineering work. Quaylane does not ship AI features; this is only about how we write code.

## Where I am

A seven-week pilot with three engineers ended on 2026-09-25 ([[ai-assistant-pilot-results-2026-09]]). Useful for tests and boilerplate, larger pull requests, and no change to the rule that a human approves every merge. The draft policy ([[ai-coding-tools-policy-draft]]) is waiting on [[should-engineers-use-ai-assistants-on-production-code]]. My working stance is [[ai-assistants-raise-the-review-bar]].
