# Churn Call With Ezra Feldman 2025-08-14

Exit call with [[ezra-feldman|Ezra Feldman]], practice manager at a family practice customer that cancelled at the end of 2025-08. Imani and I joined; 35 minutes.

## What he said

- The practice moved to a system that lets patients book online from the practice website. Their front desk had been taking about a third of calls just to book routine visits.
- Reminder costs above the 1,000 messages a month included in his plan had become a monthly argument with his owner.
- He liked the day view and said the product was easier for new front-desk staff than what they were moving to.

## What we did with it

- First churn reason in the log naming online booking. By year end four of the 23 lost accounts named it; see [[churn-analysis-2025]].
- Renata changed reminder pricing in 2025-10 to a higher included volume for small plans.
- Ezra agreed we could contact him if we ever build online booking. Noted on [[online-booking-widget]].

## My note to self

I went into the call expecting a complaint about the July slowness and heard about a feature we had decided not to build. The decision may still be right, but I had not been counting its cost in churn.
