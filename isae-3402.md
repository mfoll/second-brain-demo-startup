# ISAE 3402

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2025-12-18.

## Summary

ISAE 3402, "Assurance Reports on Controls at a Service Organization", is an international assurance standard describing Service Organization Control engagements, which give a service organization's customers assurance that it has adequate internal controls.

## Why It Matters

One prospect in 2026-Q3 asked whether we had "a SOC 2 or ISAE 3402 report". This article is how I learned they are related standards for the same kind of assurance engagement, so our SOC 2 report answered the question. Recorded so that [[dana-prescott]] can answer it without me next time ([[can-we-answer-security-questionnaires-without-engineering-time]]).

## Caveats

The article is short on how the two standards differ in practice; ask [[patrice-ndour]] if a customer insists on one specifically.
