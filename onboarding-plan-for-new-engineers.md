# Onboarding Plan For New Engineers

The first six weeks for a new engineer, as used for Ayesha (2026-03) and Bram (2026-07). Revised after each.

## Week 1

- Laptop through Colette's checklist, which is also SOC 2 evidence for onboarding.
- Read: [[team-working-agreements]], [[code-review-policy-document]], [[on-call-handbook]], the architecture overview.
- First pull request merged by day three: a real but small ticket, tagged `first-pr`.
- Meet each engineer for 30 minutes, and Felix, Imani and Renata.

## Weeks 2 to 4

- One feature ticket with a named buddy who pairs at least twice a week.
- Shadow one on-call week as an observer.
- Sit in on one customer call with customer success.

## Weeks 5 and 6

- Second on-call shadow week, as primary with the real primary as backup.
- Thirty-day conversation with me: what is confusing, what we should stop doing.

## Changes after Bram

- Platform engineers start with `quaylane-infra` access read-only for two weeks. Write access after the access review sign-off.
- Added the scheduling engine walkthrough recording ([[scheduling-engine-walkthrough-recording]]) to week 2 since 2026-09.
