# Privacy Counsel Memo On Reminder Logs

[[abigail-ferris]]'s memo of 2026-10-14 answering my written question of 2026-09-21 about how long we may keep reminder delivery logs.

## Her Points

- As processor, we keep data only as long as the clinic's instructions and the purpose require. Indefinite retention with no documented reason is hard to defend.
- A default retention period, written in the DPA security annex and justified by the purpose (dispute handling, incident investigation), is reasonable. She did not name a number; she said the reason matters more than the figure.
- Aggregated counts without contact details can be kept longer.
- Enterprise customers may set their own period by contract.

## What I Take From It

Option 3 in [[how-long-should-we-retain-reminder-message-logs]], customer-configurable with a short default, is the legally cleanest. Option 1 is acceptable if we document the reason. Decision with Imani by 2026-11-30.
