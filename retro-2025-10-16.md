# Retro 2025-10-16

Quarterly engineering retro, the one where we dropped the daily standup.

## What happened

Six engineers and me, 60 minutes, run by [[lucia-ferreyra]]. The standup came up in four of six "what drains you" cards. The complaints were concrete: it sat at 09:30, which split the morning for the two people who start early; it ran 20 to 25 minutes because problem-solving happened in it; and it mostly repeated what was already in pull requests and tickets.

[[nadia-carvalho]] was in her last month and said she had stopped attending in September without anyone noticing, which made the point better than anything I could have said.

## Decision

A six-week trial from 2025-10-20:

- Written async updates in `#eng-updates` by 10:30 each working day: yesterday, today, blocked on. Three lines.
- A 30-minute live sync on Tuesday and Thursday at 14:00 for anything that needs talking.
- Review at the retro on 2025-12-04 ([[async-updates-trial-review-2025-12-04]]).

The old format is kept for reference in [[daily-standup-format]].

## My part

I had defended the standup in the previous retro as "the only time we see each other". Lucia asked whether that was a reason for engineers or for me. Fair question; I wrote it down.
