# Deliver The Product

The roadmap, releases and product engineering for the Quaylane app, shared with [[felix-ostrowski|Felix Ostrowski]] as head of product. It has no end date: there is always a next release, a next roadmap and a next argument about scope.

## Scope

- What we build and when: the yearly roadmap, quarterly scope, the release train and launch communications with customer success.
- Product engineering on the scheduling core: appointment books, rooms and staff rosters, reminders, the waitlist and multi-location calendars. See [[appointment-scheduling]].
- The engineering side of discovery: estimates, technical spikes and feasibility notes. Felix owns the problem framing; I own the cost and the risk. See [[product-discovery]].
- Not here: architecture and reliability (under [[run-the-platform]]), the Meridale contract and rollout (under [[partner-with-customers]]), hiring and team practice (under [[lead-engineering-team]]).
