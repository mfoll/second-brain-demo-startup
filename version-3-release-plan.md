# Version 3 Release Plan

The plan for Quaylane 3.0 as agreed on 2026-01-05, with the June date. Kept unchanged as a record; the current state is in [[version-3-release]].

## Summary

- Three features: waitlist autofill, multi-location calendars, enterprise SSO.
- GA 2026-06-23. Beta for waitlist from 2026-05-04.
- Capacity: all five engineers on product from 2026-03-02, after the tenant migration pilot ends on 2026-02-27, plus the first 2026 hire from April.

## Why It Matters

It is the baseline against which the slip is measured. The capacity line is where it broke: it assumed platform work would end with the pilot. Reviewed in [[lessons-from-the-version-3-slip]].

## Key Details

| Feature | Owner | Estimate | Planned done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Waitlist autofill | Hamish | 14 weeks | 2026-05-29 |
| Multi-location calendars | Lucia | 16 weeks | 2026-06-12 |
| Enterprise SSO | Min-jae | 12 weeks | 2026-06-05 |

No contingency line. Risks listed: Meridale identity provider readiness, calendar performance. Not listed: the tenant migration.

## Caveats

Estimates per feature were close to actual effort; the dates failed on capacity. SSO ended up taking 15 weeks of Min-jae's time but started later than planned.
