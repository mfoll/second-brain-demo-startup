# What Should The 2027 Roadmap Cut?

## Question

Draft v2 of the roadmap asks for about 60 engineer-weeks of product themes against about 46 available, assuming the platform reservation stays at 30% and the two hires start in 2027-Q2. What goes?

## Options on the table

- **Drop or defer self-booking** (20 weeks). Frees the most, but leaves the top request unanswered for another year.
- **Shrink clinic group operations to reporting only** (24 down to 12). Meridale wants the room report; a second enterprise customer is not signed.
- **Shrink recall and physio** (16 down to 8): recall reminders only, no group bookings.
- **Cut the platform reservation to 20%.** I do not want this; the reservation covers on-call, SOC 2 Type II and the row-level security work.

## Constraints

- The hiring decision changes the arithmetic: a product engineer adds about 8 weeks of roadmap capacity in 2027, a platform engineer adds about 3 and gives back on-call time instead.
- Felix and I agreed to cut whole themes before thinning all of them.
