# Psychological safety

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-04.

## Summary

Psychological safety means people expect no punishment or embarrassment when they raise an idea, ask a question, voice a worry or admit a mistake. In a team, members feel able to take social risks without being made to look foolish. The article describes it as the most studied enabling condition in research on group dynamics and team learning.

## Why It Matters

The idea behind the survey question "I can disagree openly in meetings" ([[team-health-survey-2026-06]], 4.0 of 5). Two moments this year test it: Hamish's recorded dissent at [[engineering-meeting-2026-04-08]], which I want to stay normal rather than exceptional, and the version 3 slip, where two engineers knew the date was wrong and did not say so in a meeting ([[coaching-reflection-2026-06]]). The second one is on me.
