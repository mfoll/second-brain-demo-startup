# Working With Felix On Product

How Felix Ostrowski and I split product and engineering decisions, written after our first year together so that the team and Renata can see it. Felix read and agreed to it on 2025-12-02.

## The split

- **Felix decides** which problems we solve, for whom, and in what order, within the capacity I give him.
- **I decide** how we build, what it costs, and how much capacity goes to platform, security and on-call.
- **We decide together** the scope cut when the two collide, and the hire mix. If we cannot agree, Renata decides, and we have used that twice.

## Habits that work

- A Monday sync with a written agenda. Decisions go into the vault or the ticket the same day.
- Disagreements in writing before the meeting. Our best calls (waitlist matching, the 3.0 slip) came out of a one-page argument each.
- Felix joins at least one engineering design review a month; I join at least one customer interview a month.

## Where it strains

- Hiring. Felix wants a product engineer as the first 2027 hire; I am waiting for the paging data. The disagreement is recorded in [[should-we-hire-a-platform-engineer-or-keep-everyone-on-call]].
- Platform reservations look like lost roadmap capacity to him. I owe him a clearer account of what the 30% buys.
