# How Much Should We Spend On Security In 2027?

What belongs in the 2027 security and compliance line, and how big should it be relative to engineering headcount?

## Known Costs

- SOC 2 Type II examination: Ashcombe's indicative fee $38,000 (to be confirmed by 2026-12-15).
- Annual penetration test: $14,000 in 2026; budgeting $16,000.
- MDM and endpoint tools for 14 to 16 laptops: about $4,800.
- Privacy counsel hours: about $8,000, mostly DPAs for enterprise prospects.
- Draft total about $71,000 ([[engineering-budget-2027-draft]]).

## Open Points

- A compliance automation platform would add $12,000 to $20,000 a year ([[should-we-buy-a-compliance-automation-platform]]).
- ISO 27001 would add certification costs if enterprise prospects ask for it ([[do-we-need-iso-27001-as-well-as-soc-2]]).
- Whether a share of a platform engineer's time should be counted here.

## Leaning

Keep the line to known recurring costs plus a contingency of $10,000, and fund tools only if they save measurable engineering time. Decide with Renata by 2026-11-13.
