# Minimum viable product

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-07-16.

## Summary

Defines a minimum viable product as a version of a product with just enough features to be used by early customers, who then give feedback that shapes later development.

## Why It Matters

The argument over the [[online-booking-widget]] is mostly an argument about what "minimum" means. Felix's minimum is one service at one site; Joanne Pruett's minimum includes the front desk holding slots back. The [[booking-widget-estimate]] prices both, and the link-based version for returning patients is my candidate for the smallest thing worth learning from.

## Caveats

For a public booking surface, the security work is not optional in any version, minimal or not.
