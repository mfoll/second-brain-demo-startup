# System And Organization Controls

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-09-15.

## Summary

The article describes System and Organization Controls as the AICPA's name for a suite of reports produced during an audit, intended for service organizations to issue validated reports on the internal controls over the information systems they provide to their customers.

## Why It Matters

It is the quick explanation I send to new hires and to clinic customers who see "SOC 2" in our contract and ask what it is. It frames SOC reports as something a customer uses to judge a service provider, which is the right frame for us: Meridale asked for our report so they did not have to audit us themselves ([[meridale-security-questionnaire]]).

## Caveats

An encyclopedia overview. For the criteria and report types, the AICPA material ([[aicpa-soc-suite-of-services]]) and our own notes ([[trust-services-criteria]], [[soc2-type-1-vs-type-2]]) are more precise.
