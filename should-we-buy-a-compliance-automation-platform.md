# Should We Buy A Compliance Automation Platform?

Compliance automation platforms connect to AWS, GitHub and the HR system, collect evidence automatically and track controls. Several vendors pitched us in 2026-02.

## History

In 2026-02 I decided against one for the Type I: annual prices quoted were $12,000 to $20,000, setup would take weeks we did not have, and Patrice Ndour said a well-kept spreadsheet and evidence folder were enough for a company of 14 ([[a-spreadsheet-is-enough-for-a-first-soc-2]]). The Type I went through on the [[soc2-control-matrix]].

## Why It Is Open Again

The Type II needs evidence every month, not once. Collecting it by hand costs me about 4 hours a month and Colette about 3. The two Type I exceptions were both recurring controls that nobody was reminded of, which is exactly what these tools are good at.

## What Would Decide It

- Hours actually spent on evidence from 2026-09 to 2026-12 (tracking in the [[type-2-observation-tracker]]).
- Whether any vendor can pull GitHub review records and the access review without manual work.
- The 2027 security line ([[how-much-should-we-spend-on-security-in-2027]]).

Decision target: with the 2027 budget, 2026-11-13. No change of tool during the Type II window regardless.
