# Kano model

Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-10-19.

## Summary

Describes the Kano model, a theory of product development and customer satisfaction from the 1980s that sorts customer preferences about features into five categories, each affecting satisfaction differently, to help decide which development efforts matter most.

## Why It Matters

Mai brought it to the 2026-08 product review as a way to read the [[customer-feedback-log]]. Reminders and overlap protection are must-haves: nobody asks for them, everyone leaves without them. Waitlist autofill turned out to be an attractive feature: nobody missed it before, and beta clinics did not want to give it back. It helped me explain [[feedback-volume-is-not-priority]] to the team.

## Caveats

We used it as a vocabulary, not with the questionnaire the method prescribes.
