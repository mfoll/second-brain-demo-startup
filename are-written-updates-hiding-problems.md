# Are Written Updates Hiding Problems?

After we replaced the daily standup with [[written-async-updates]] in 2025-10, I worried that people would write "on track" and sit on a blocker for days.

## Current Answer

Answered for now, no. I checked twice: at the trial review ([[async-updates-trial-review-2025-12-04]]) and in 2026-06. In both samples, blockers appeared in the written update within a day in most cases, and the Thursday "stuck for more than a day" round caught the rest. The one blocker that sat for four days in 2026-05 was mine: I had not answered a question in a thread.

## Revisit

If a new hire stays quiet in updates during their first month, which is a known risk. Watched with Bram in 2026-07; not an issue.
