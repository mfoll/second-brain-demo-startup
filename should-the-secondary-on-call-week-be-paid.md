# Should The Secondary On-Call Week Be Paid?

The $300 stipend covers only the primary week. The secondary is expected to be reachable within 15 minutes all week but is paid nothing extra. Is that fair, and does it matter?

## Current Answer

Open. Arguments so far:

- For paying: the secondary keeps the same constraints on evenings and weekends as the primary. [[min-jae-oh]] raised it in the 2026-06 survey comments.
- Against: the secondary was paged 9 times in all of Q3 across the rotation ([[paging-report-2026-q3]]), and paying both weeks doubles the cost to about $31,200 a year.
- A middle option: $75 for the secondary week, or $150 per secondary page out of hours. [[colette-fairbanks]] prefers a flat amount for payroll simplicity.

## Next step

Put it into the [[engineering-budget-2027]] as a line with two options, and decide together with [[should-we-hire-a-platform-engineer-or-keep-everyone-on-call]], since a platform engineer would change who is secondary.
