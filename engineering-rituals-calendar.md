# Engineering Rituals Calendar

Every recurring engineering meeting and written ritual as of 2026-10, with its cost in people-hours a month. I review this list each January and drop anything nobody would miss.

| Ritual | When | Who | Hours a month (team) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Written updates ([[written-async-updates]]) | Daily by 10:30 | All engineers | about 6 |
| Tuesday and Thursday sync | 14:00, 30 min | All engineers | about 30 |
| Wednesday engineering meeting | Weekly, 45 min, decisions only | All engineers | about 24 |
| On-call handoff note ([[on-call-handbook]]) | Monday 10:00 | Outgoing primary | under 1 |
| One-to-ones ([[one-on-one-notes-folder]]) | Every second week, 30 min | Each engineer and me | about 7 |
| Retro ([[retro-notes-archive]]) | Every six weeks, 60 min | All engineers | about 5 |
| Release cut ([[release-train-handover-2026-07-14]]) | Tuesday | Lucia | about 3 |
| Friday coffee call | Optional, 30 min | Whoever comes | about 2 |

## Notes

- The Wednesday meeting is the one I would cut first if load rises; most weeks it could be a written decision log.
- Total is roughly 75 people-hours a month for eight people, about 6% of working time. In 2025, with the daily standup, it was about 9%.
