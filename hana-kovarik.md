# Hana Kovarik

Scheduling coordinator at a multi-site imaging center, a customer since 2024-05 (tenant 1188, four sites). Her account was the one hit hardest by the March outage.

## Context

Her center sends more reminders per day than most of our accounts because imaging appointments need preparation instructions. On 2026-03-17 412 of their reminders were delayed or not sent, the largest number of any clinic.

## How We Work With Her

- [[pablo-arriaga]] is her main contact. I joined one call with her on 2026-03-20 to explain what happened and what we were changing.
- She asked two concrete things: a list of reminders that were not sent, so her team could phone people, and a warning within 15 minutes next time. The first became postmortem action 8 (the unsent reminders report); the second fed the queue-age alert and the status page.
- She has been a useful early tester since: she checked the unsent reminders report in staging in 2026-05.

## Current Threads
