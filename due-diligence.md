# Due Diligence

English Wikipedia article, last edited 2026-08-14.

## Summary

Due diligence is the investigation or care that a reasonable business or person is normally expected to take before entering an agreement or contract with another party. The article covers its uses in transactions, including investment.

## Why It Matters

Read while answering Arun's request list ([[copperwell-diligence-request-list]]). The useful framing: diligence is what a careful investor is expected to do, not a sign of distrust. That changed how I wrote the answers: plain, with evidence, and without defending decisions that do not need defending. My own working definition for the engineering part is in [[technical-diligence]].

## Caveats

General article; no guidance on technical diligence for software companies specifically.
