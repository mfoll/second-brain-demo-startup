# Site reliability engineering

## Summary

Wikipedia article on SRE: a discipline that monitors and improves the availability and performance of deployed software, with emphasis on automation and infrastructure as code, and overlapping with DevOps in its goals.

## Why It Matters

Shared after the [[postmortem-review-2026-03-24]]. We are not going to have an SRE team; I wanted the vocabulary (service levels, toil, error budgets) available when we argue about reliability versus roadmap. It also frames [[kwabena-asare]]'s case in [[should-we-hire-a-platform-engineer-or-keep-everyone-on-call]].
