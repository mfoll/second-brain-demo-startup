# How Legal Awareness Shapes Developers' Data Compliance Behavior: A Deterrence Theory Perspective

Wang X, Zhang Z. PLoS One, 2026.

## Summary

A survey of software developers analyzed with structural equation modelling. The authors find that developers' awareness of data law is associated with more compliant data handling, both directly and through how certain and how severe they perceive sanctions to be, with perceived certainty mattering more than severity. A perceived compliance culture in the organization strengthens the link between legal awareness and perceived certainty.

## Why It Matters

I read it while planning the 2027 training ([[security-awareness-training-records]]). The certainty finding fits our experience: engineers changed behavior around review once branch protection made a missing approval visible every time, not when we explained the audit. It argues for making data handling rules checkable (scrubbing rules in the error tracker, retention jobs) rather than relying on a training session about the data protection law.

## Caveats

A survey of self-reported behavior in one national regulatory context, not an intervention study. It does not say what training content works, and our team of eight is not a population.
